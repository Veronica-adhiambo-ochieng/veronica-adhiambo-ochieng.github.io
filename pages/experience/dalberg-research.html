Overview

I spent over four years at Dalberg Research SEZ Limited, a research firm in Nairobi. I started by building the digital surveys that field teams used to collect data, then moved into data analysis and machine learning. In 2022 my colleagues voted me Employee of the Year.

What I did as a Data Analyst

  • Built machine learning models in Keras for voice detection and audio quality checks, which made data processing 40% more efficient.
  • Built an OCR and CNN model in TensorFlow and Keras that reads nutrition information from photos, with 84% accuracy.
  • Automated data collection and processing using Python, web scraping and data mining.
  • Built interactive dashboards to share research findings and support decisions.
  • Analysed quantitative and qualitative data, including qualitative coding, thematic analysis and socioeconomic trend analysis, and wrote up the findings in reports.

What I did as a Survey Scripter

  • Designed and scripted digital data collection tools, including research questionnaires.
  • Tested and refined the scripts so data was captured accurately and fieldwork ran smoothly.
  • Trained field teams on the tools, survey procedures and data quality requirements.
  • Checked data quality and prepared datasets for analysis.

Tools
