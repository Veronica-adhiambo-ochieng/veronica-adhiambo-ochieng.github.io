Overview

As a Research Trainee with Dr. Cara Pager at the RNA Institute, University at Albany, I led the computational analysis for a project on Deer Tick virus.

The project

We don't know much about how Deer Tick virus changes human cells at the RNA level. Does it change which genes are switched on, or how the cell edits its RNA (splicing)?

Data

  • RNA-sequencing data from human lung cells (A549) infected with the virus.

What I did

  • Designed and ran the whole analysis pipeline, from processing the raw RNA-seq data to the final results.
  • Measured changes in gene expression with DESeq2.
  • Measured changes in RNA splicing with rMATS.
  • Used gene ontology analysis to link the changes to biological pathways.
  • Presented the findings to researchers at the RNA Institute.

What I found

The virus affects the host cell mainly through RNA splicing, rather than through overall gene expression. Gene ontology analysis pointed to the biological pathways linked to the host's response.

Tools

  • RNA-seq analysis: processing the sequencing data.
  • DESeq2: differential gene expression.
  • rMATS: alternative splicing.
  • Gene ontology analysis: linking results to biology.
