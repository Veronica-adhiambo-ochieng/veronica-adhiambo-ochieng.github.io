Overview

MESO+ is an AI-powered maternal mental health platform. It helps mothers notice when they're struggling and connects them to the right care. I founded it and lead the technical side.

The problem

Most mothers go through emotional changes during pregnancy and after birth. When those changes go unsupported, they can turn into postpartum depression. And even then, many mothers walk that journey alone.

The gap

The help already exists: psychologists, counsellors and nurses that mothers could talk to. But mothers tend to cope quietly, and check-ups focus on the pregnancy and the baby. The system is there. What's missing is the connection between the mother and the help.

How MESO+ fills it

MESO+ connects the mother to the care she needs. She can reach out from her phone, and her nurse can see how each of her mothers is doing and step in early.

Where AI helps

Learning when she needs it

If a mother wants to understand something about her body, her baby or her feelings, AI explains it in plain words.

A quiet heads-up

If a mother is struggling but isn't ready to tell her provider, AI sends the provider a short report so they know to check in.

Seeing change over time

AI gives a regular overview of how each mother is doing: what's changing in her life and what's helping her feel better.
