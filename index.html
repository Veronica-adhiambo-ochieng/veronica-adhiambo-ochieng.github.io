Hi, I’m Veronica, and I like building things that help people get care before it’s too late. I’m a mathematical epidemiologist working where statistics, machine learning and software meet health. I enjoy turning complex data into tools that people on the ground can actually use.

Currently, I’m a Research Assistant at the Swiss Tropical and Public Health Institute, where I co-developed MalReBay, a Bayesian R package that tells malaria treatment failures apart from new infections, helping researchers measure how well antimalarial drugs are working.

I’m also the founder and technical lead of MESO+, an AI-powered maternal mental health platform that helps mothers notice when they’re struggling and connects them to the right care.

AI is part of how I work every day. I use it to break down complex ideas, build solutions faster and keep learning, and I spend a lot of time reading about AI policy and how it can be put into practice in health systems like the ones I work in.

Outside work, I’m usually watching anime, catching a movie, or playing sport: lawn tennis, badminton, ping pong, and the occasional fencing bout. I also run for fun to unwind. Fun fact: I represented my school in badminton at national championships.

If you’re passionate about turning data into decisions, or want to talk about how AI can help us build better tools for health, I’d love to chat. Email me at veronica.adhiambo.ochieng@gmail.com.
